    99103734 (Patreon) 
    Hey Daniel this error usually happens when a Blueprint Struct was changed, renamed, or corrupted and Unreal still has old references cached.
    Published: 2026-05
    99103734 (Patreon) 
    Try opening the PlayerSettings, GameplaySettings, and Gameplay_Player structs, then compile and save each one. This usually refreshes broken struct references and can fix the “Unknown structure” packaging error.
    Published: 2026-05
    99103734 (Patreon) 
    After that, just recompile the Blueprints related to those structs and hit Save All. Then make blackup the project then close unreal engine and delete the Binaries, Intermediate, DerivedDataCache, and Saved folders from your project folder. Once you reopen the project, right click the content folder and choose “Fix Up Redirectors.” That usually clears up the “Unknown structure” packaging error.
